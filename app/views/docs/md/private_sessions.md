## Offering a private session

Private sessions are one-to-one or small group sessions you offer by arrangement rather than at a set time, like bodywork, massage, meditation, breathwork or coaching. They're listed on the [Private sessions](/private_sessions) page, which you can find in the sidebar under Facilitators.

- Click 'Private sessions' in the sidebar, then 'Offer a private session'
- Give it a name and tags, like massage or meditation, say what happens in a session and how people book it, and whether you offer it in person, online or both
- Enter where you offer it, the maximum number of people (1 for one-to-one), and optionally its length in minutes and price
- Click 'Create private session'

Its URL fills in from its name as you type it, like /ps/thai-massage, and you can change it, as long as no other session has it. <!-- claim: slug-from-name --> A session offered in person needs a location; an online one has none. <!-- claim: in-person-needs-location --> A session is for one person or more, up to the most you'll see at once, or as many as come if you leave the number blank. <!-- claim: group-size --> Its length is a number of minutes or a range, like 60-90, and its price a number or a range for a sliding scale, like 40-80, or nothing if you leave it blank. <!-- claim: length-and-price -->

Your private sessions are also listed on your profile. To keep one off the Private sessions page and your profile, tick 'Secret': only people with its link will see it. You still see it under 'My private sessions' on the Private sessions page, marked Secret. <!-- claim: unlisted -->

Only you (and Dandelion's admins) can edit or delete your private sessions. <!-- claim: only-owner-edits -->

## Vouching for a private session

If you know a private session is good, click Vouch on it, and add a testimonial if you like. You can vouch for a session once, and not for your own. <!-- claim: vouch-once-not-own -->

Your name and picture show with your vouch and testimonial on the session, and lists show how many people have vouched for it. You can change your testimonial or remove your vouch there too. <!-- claim: vouch-shown-changed -->

## Booking a private session

Private sessions aren't booked through Dandelion: a session can say how to book it, with the email address, phone number or booking link the person offering it gives, and you arrange a time and payment with them directly. <!-- claim: how-to-book -->

You can search private sessions by name and description, filter them by tag and by in person or online (sessions offered both ways show under either), and find those near a place. <!-- claim: search-filters -->

The sessions most vouched for are listed first, or the newest first if you sort by Most recently created. <!-- claim: sort-by-vouches -->
